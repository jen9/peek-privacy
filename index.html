Privacy Policy

What the Greedle app collects, why, and what it does not collect. Written from what the app actually does, not from a template.

The short version

The app works without an account. Reading the Fear & Greed gauges, the macro series and the events calendar needs no sign-in and no personal data at all.

An account is needed only to carry paid unlocks between your devices. If you create one, the only personal information involved is your email address.

There is no advertising network, no analytics service, and no third party receiving your data.

What is collected

An installation identifier

A random identifier is generated on your device the first time the app runs, stored in your device's secure storage, and sent with each request to our service.

It is generated by the app, not taken from your phone or your operating system, and it identifies an installation rather than a person. It exists so that a reader poll can be limited to one vote per installation and so that faults can be traced. Reinstalling the app produces a new one.

Your email address, if you create an account

Creating an account requires an email address and a password. They are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. The app itself never stores your password, and our service never receives it in a readable form.

Your email address is used to sign you in, to recover your account if you forget your password, and to attach purchases to you so that they appear on any device you sign in to.

A notification token, if you turn alerts on

If you allow notifications, your device issues a push token which is sent to our service together with which alert types you have enabled. It is used only to deliver the alerts you asked for. Turning alerts off stops it being used.

On Android the token is issued by Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which is how every Android app receives notifications; there is no other route. Google therefore knows that this installation can receive notifications from this app. Nothing else about you is sent to Firebase: no account, no email address, and none of the figures the app shows.

Purchases

Purchases are taken by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details — the stores handle payment entirely and tell us only whether a purchase happened.

When you buy something, the app sends the store's purchase token to our service, which asks the store to confirm it. We record which store took the payment, the store's own identifier for it, what was bought, and which account holds it.

The purchase token itself is deliberately not stored. It is a credential, it is not needed once the purchase has been confirmed, and keeping it would put it somewhere it could later leak.

What is not collected

  • Location, of any kind, precise or approximate
  • Contacts, photos, files, calendar or any other content on your device
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browsing or app-usage analytics
  • Any profile of you, your interests, or your finances

The app holds no funds, places no orders, and connects to no brokerage. The market figures it shows are informational and are not investment advice.

There is no advertising network and no analytics provider. Promotions shown inside the app are for the app's own paid features and involve no third party.

Diagnostics

The app and our service record faults so they can be fixed. Those records are automatically stripped of credentials — tokens, receipts, passwords and email addresses are removed both by field name and by scanning the text of the message itself, so that a credential cannot arrive by accident inside an error's own wording.

Keeping data

Your account and its purchases are kept while the account exists, because a one-time unlock you paid for must still be there when you sign in on a new phone.

Alert preferences and the notification token are kept while alerts are on.

The market figures the app stores on your device — the reading behind a home-screen widget — stay on the device and are overwritten as newer ones arrive. They are never uploaded.

Your choices

Use the app without an account. Everything except the paid unlocks works with no account and no personal data.

Turn alerts off at any time in Profile, which stops the notification token being used.

Sign out, which ends the session on this device and on our service.

Delete your account and everything attached to it, from inside the app: Profile → Delete my account. It asks you to confirm, tells you what will be lost, and then removes the account, its purchases and its alert settings straight away. You are signed out on every device. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it.

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, write to the address below from the account's email address. The account is deleted within 30 days and you will be told when it is done.

Deleting the account does not refund purchases and does not cancel anything with the store. Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store, who took the payment.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
